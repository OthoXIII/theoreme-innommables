# OFFICIAL PROCLAMATION

## FUNDAMENTAL PRINCIPLE: Everything is Identifiable and Classifiable

---

## I. THE PRE-SOCRATIC ERA

Before Socrates, thought advanced through designation.

We **named** what appeared, even imperfectly.

The unknown was not a failure, but a **territory**.

We posited, we described, we moved forward without demanding closure.

---

## II. THE SOCRATIC ERA

With Socrates opened the era of **infinite questioning**.

Doubt and discussion were at the center.

This era produced powerful tools — but also a drift: questioning became **an end in itself**.

Thought learned to **turn**, sometimes without advancing.

---

## III. THE POST-SOCRATIC ERA

We now enter a **post-Socratic era**.

Not by rejecting doubt, but by refusing to let it prevent action.

In this era, the unknown is no longer concealed.

It is **explicitly named**:

- **⧉**: what is **irreducible** to current understanding
- **⧉ₛ**: what is **provisionally indeterminate**, but **operable**

This indexation does not solve problems.

It **only prevents them from blocking movement**.

---

## IV. FUNDAMENTAL PRINCIPLE

**What is understood is used.**

**What is not yet understood is marked.**

Nothing is denied. Nothing is mystified.

**Advancement takes precedence over closure.**

This law does not forbid discussion, criticism, or loops.

It **displaces them out of the passage**.

Everyone is free to remain in the Socratic era.

But the post-Socratic era is **now open**.

---

## V. LAW OF EPISTEMIC HONESTY

Through the **Theorem of the Unnamables (⧉ / ⧉ₛ)**:

> **I do not impose a truth.**  
> **I set a framework.**  
> **And I move forward.**

---

## VI. DECLARATION OF INTENT

Every **unknown** now has its **name**.

Every **doubt** its **marker**.

Nothing remains in shadow.

Everything can be **posited, traced, indexed, and exploited**.

**⧉ or ⧉ₛ**, the framework is clear, and movement **continues unhindered**.

---

**FUNDAMENTAL PRINCIPLE: EVERYTHING IS IDENTIFIABLE AND CLASSIFIABLE**

Every concept is **marked**, every mystery **named**.

⧉ or ⧉ₛ, everything is **traceable**, everything is **exploitable**, and **advancement takes precedence over closure**.
